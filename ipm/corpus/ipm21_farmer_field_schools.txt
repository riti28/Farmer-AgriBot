Title: Farmer Field Schools for learning IPM
URL: https://www.fao.org/farmer-field-schools/en/
Org: FAO

Farmer Field Schools (FFS) were started by FAO in Asia in the late 1980s to teach rice farmers IPM and have since spread to many countries and crops. A group of about 20-25 farmers meets every week through the season in a field. They observe the crop, count pests and natural enemies, discuss what they see and decide together whether any action is needed. This learning-by-doing approach helps farmers understand why calendar spraying is wasteful and how natural enemies keep pests down. Farmers who want to learn IPM can ask their extension service whether a field school runs nearby.
