Title: Identifying fall armyworm in maize
URL: https://iimr.icar.gov.in/
Org: ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR)

Fall armyworm (FAW) is an invasive caterpillar first reported in India from Karnataka in 2018. It mainly attacks maize but also sorghum and other crops. The larva has an inverted Y mark on the head and four dark spots arranged in a square on the second last body segment. Young larvae scrape leaves and make window-pane patches; older larvae make ragged holes and leave sawdust-like droppings in the whorl. Moths fly long distances and lay egg masses covered with scales on leaves. Early detection is important: scout maize from seedling stage and check about 50 plants per acre in several spots.
