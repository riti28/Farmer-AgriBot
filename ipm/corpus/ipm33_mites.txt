Title: Spider mites and other mites
URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/
Org: University of California Statewide IPM Program (UC IPM)

Mites are not insects; they are tiny eight-legged animals related to spiders, often too small to see without a hand lens. Spider mites live under leaves, sometimes under fine webbing, and suck cell contents, causing fine yellow or white speckling, bronzing and drying of leaves. They multiply very fast in hot, dry, dusty conditions. Many ordinary insecticides do not kill mites and can make mite problems worse by killing predatory mites and other natural enemies. Keep plants well watered, reduce dust on field roads, remove badly infested leaves and use a miticide (acaricide) or wettable sulphur only if the local agriculture officer recommends it.
