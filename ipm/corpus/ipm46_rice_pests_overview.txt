Title: Main pests of rice and how to recognise them
URL: https://www.icar-iirr.org/
Org: ICAR-Indian Institute of Rice Research (IIRR)

Common insect pests of rice include: stem borers (dead hearts in young plants and white empty earheads later), brown planthopper and white-backed planthopper (insects at the base of plants, hopper burn patches), leaf folder (folded leaves with white streaks), gall midge (silver shoots), green leafhopper (spreads tungro virus, causing yellow-orange stunted plants), rice hispa (white scraping and tunnels in leaves) and rice bugs (empty or spotted grains at milky stage). Rats also damage rice. Main diseases are blast, sheath blight, bacterial leaf blight and tungro. Correct identification matters, because each pest has its own action threshold and management, and many leaf-feeding insects do not reduce yield enough to need spraying.
