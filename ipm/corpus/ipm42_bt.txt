Title: Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis) bio-insecticide
URL: https://ipm.ucanr.edu/
Org: University of California Statewide IPM Program (UC IPM)

Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) is a soil bacterium that produces proteins toxic to certain insects. When a caterpillar eats leaves sprayed with Bt, the protein damages its gut and it stops feeding within hours and dies in a few days. The common kurstaki type kills caterpillars (larvae of moths and butterflies) such as armyworms, loopers, diamondback moth and bollworms, but it is safe for people, animals, bees and most natural enemies. Bt works best on young, small larvae, so spray when eggs hatch, cover the leaves well and spray in the evening because sunlight breaks it down. Bt does not work on sucking pests like aphids or whiteflies. Bt cotton and Bt maize contain the same type of protein inside the plant.
