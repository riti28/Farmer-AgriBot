Title: Cotton IPM in India (example from north Karnataka)
URL: https://uasraichur.edu.in/
Org: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Raichur

In north Karnataka districts such as Raichur, Dharwad and Belagavi, cotton faces sucking pests early (aphids, jassids, thrips, whitefly) and bollworms later. Seed treatment, border crops such as maize or sorghum, and yellow sticky traps are used against sucking pests. Growing a refuge of non-Bt cotton around Bt cotton slows resistance. Farmers are advised to avoid spraying in the first 60 days unless a threshold is crossed, because early sprays kill lady beetles, lacewings and spiders. Community action, where all farmers in a village destroy crop residues and stop the crop at the same time, gives much better control of pink bollworm than single-farm action.
