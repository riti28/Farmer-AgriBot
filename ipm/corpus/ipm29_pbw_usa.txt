Title: Pink bollworm eradication in the United States
URL: https://www.aphis.usda.gov/
Org: USDA Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS)

Pink bollworm was a major cotton pest in the south-western United States and northern Mexico for decades. A cooperative eradication programme combined pheromone trap monitoring, mating disruption with pheromones, Bt cotton, mandatory crop destruction after harvest, and releases of huge numbers of sterile moths that mate with wild moths but produce no offspring. Using all these tactics together across the whole region, the USDA declared the pest eradicated from US cotton-growing areas in 2018. The programme shows how area-wide, community action with many IPM tools can succeed where single-farm spraying fails.
