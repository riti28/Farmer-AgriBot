Title: Pheromone traps, light traps and sticky traps
URL: https://ppqs.gov.in/
Org: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (Govt. of India)

Traps help farmers know when a pest arrives and how many there are. Pheromone traps use a synthetic sex smell to attract male moths of one species, for example rice stem borer, fall armyworm or pink bollworm. Lures must be changed every 2-4 weeks. Light traps attract many night-flying insects and are useful for community monitoring. Yellow sticky traps catch whiteflies and aphids; blue ones catch thrips. Trap counts should be recorded every few days; a sudden rise warns the farmer to scout the crop closely. In some cases, many traps per acre (mass trapping) also reduce pest numbers.
