Title: International Code of Conduct on Pesticide Management
URL: https://www.fao.org/pest-and-pesticide-management/pesticide-risk-reduction/code-conduct/en/
Org: FAO and WHO

The International Code of Conduct on Pesticide Management, published by FAO and WHO, is a global framework that guides governments, industry and users on the safe handling of pesticides over their whole life cycle. It promotes IPM, asks countries to register and regulate pesticides, and recommends that highly hazardous pesticides be phased out where they cannot be used safely. For farmers, the key messages are to use only registered products, follow the label, wear protective equipment, store pesticides away from food, children and water, and dispose of empty containers safely.
