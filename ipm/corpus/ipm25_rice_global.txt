Title: Rice planthoppers and stem borers in Asia
URL: https://www.knowledgebank.irri.org/
Org: International Rice Research Institute (IRRI) Rice Knowledge Bank

Across rice-growing Asia, including the Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and India, brown planthopper and stem borers are among the most damaging insects. IRRI stresses that planthopper outbreaks are often triggered by early-season insecticide sprays, which kill spiders and other predators and let hoppers multiply without control. Its advice: do not spray against leaf-feeding insects in the first 40 days after transplanting, grow resistant varieties, avoid excessive nitrogen and plant at the same time as neighbours. For stem borers, rice plants can compensate for some early dead hearts, so small damage in the vegetative stage rarely reduces yield.
