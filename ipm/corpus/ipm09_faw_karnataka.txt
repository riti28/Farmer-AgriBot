Title: Fall armyworm advisory for maize farmers (example from Karnataka, India)
URL: https://www.uasbangalore.edu.in/
Org: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Bengaluru

Karnataka maize growers are advised to start monitoring for fall armyworm within a week of germination. Pheromone traps (about 5 per acre) show when moths arrive. Planting border rows of a trap crop such as napier grass and intercropping with pulses reduce damage. When about 5-10% of young plants show fresh leaf damage, a neem seed kernel extract (5%) or azadirachtin spray is suggested for early larvae; for heavier attack, a recommended insecticide is sprayed into the whorl in the early morning or evening. Farmers should rotate insecticides with different modes of action and never mix many chemicals together. Ploughing after harvest exposes pupae to birds and sun.
