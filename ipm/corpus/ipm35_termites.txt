Title: Termites in field crops
URL: https://tnau.ac.in/
Org: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)

Termites (white ants) live in colonies in the soil and feed on roots and stems of crops such as sugarcane, wheat, maize and groundnut, and on young tree seedlings. Attacked plants wilt and dry, and pulled-out plants show eaten roots and soil-filled tunnels. Damage is worst in dry, sandy soils and under water stress. IPM steps: use only well-decomposed farmyard manure, because raw manure attracts termites; remove crop stubble and wood debris; irrigate in time; and destroy mounds near fields where possible. Sett or seed treatment with a recommended insecticide, or soil application of bio-agents such as Metarhizium, gives protection where termites are a known problem.
