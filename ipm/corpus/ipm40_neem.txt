Title: Neem products in pest management
URL: https://ncipm.icar.gov.in/
Org: ICAR-National Centre for Integrated Pest Management (NCIPM)

Neem seed kernels contain azadirachtin and other compounds that make insects stop feeding, disturb their moulting and reduce egg laying. Neem products are safer for people and most natural enemies than many synthetic insecticides. Farmers can make neem seed kernel extract (NSKE, usually 5%) by soaking crushed dried kernels overnight in water, filtering and adding a little soap. Neem oil is mixed with water and an emulsifier (soap) before spraying. Commercial azadirachtin formulations (for example 300, 1500 or 10000 ppm) should be used at the label dose. Neem works best against young larvae and soft-bodied pests such as aphids, whiteflies, jassids and leaf miners, and when sprayed early, in the evening, and repeated after about a week. It acts slowly and will not stop a very heavy outbreak. Neem cake applied to soil also helps against some soil pests and nematodes.
