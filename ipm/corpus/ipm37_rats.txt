Title: Rats and rodents in crops and stores
URL: https://ppqs.gov.in/
Org: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (Govt. of India)

Rats damage rice, wheat, sugarcane, groundnut and coconut in the field and eat or spoil stored grain. Signs include cut tillers, gnawed stems, burrows on bunds, and droppings. Rodent control works best when the whole village acts together at the same time, because rats move between fields. Keep bunds narrow and free of weeds, remove crop residues where rats hide, and store grain in rat-proof bins. Owls and other birds of prey eat rats, so perches and owl boxes help. Burrows can be counted and closed in the evening; burrows reopened the next morning are active. Poison baits, if used, must be placed only in active burrows or bait stations, as advised by the agriculture department, to keep children, pets and other animals safe.
