Title: Finger millet blast in East Africa and South Asia
URL: https://www.icrisat.org/
Org: ICRISAT (International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics)

Finger millet is a staple grain in East Africa (Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, Tanzania) and South Asia (India, Nepal). Blast is the main disease of the crop in both regions and can destroy much of the grain when neck and finger blast occur in wet weather. Research centres such as ICRISAT work with national programmes to screen finger millet lines for blast resistance and share resistant varieties. General IPM advice is the same everywhere: plant resistant local varieties, use clean or treated seed, rotate crops, remove infected residues and avoid sowing so late that flowering falls in the wettest period.
