Title: Leaf folder and gall midge in rice
URL: https://www.icar-iirr.org/
Org: ICAR-Indian Institute of Rice Research (IIRR)

Rice leaf folder larvae fold the leaf lengthwise and scrape the green tissue, leaving white streaks. Gall midge causes a hollow tubular shoot called a silver shoot or onion leaf. Both are more common in humid kharif weather. For leaf folder, avoid close spacing and heavy nitrogen, and pass a rope over the crop to dislodge larvae. Natural enemies such as Trichogramma chilonis and many spiders keep leaf folder under control in an unsprayed field. For gall midge, the best tool is a resistant variety suitable for the area and timely planting. Spray only if damaged leaves exceed the local action level, as advised by the agriculture officer.
