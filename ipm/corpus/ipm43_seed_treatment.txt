Title: Seed treatment: protecting the crop from day one
URL: https://ppqs.gov.in/
Org: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (Govt. of India)

Seed treatment means coating seeds with a fungicide, insecticide or bio-agent before sowing. It is one of the cheapest IPM steps because a small amount of product protects the young crop against seed-borne and soil-borne diseases, and early sucking or soil pests. Bio-agents such as Trichoderma and Pseudomonas fluorescens and bio-fertilisers such as Rhizobium or Azospirillum can also be applied to seed. The usual order is fungicide first, then insecticide, then bio-agents or bio-fertilisers just before sowing; check product compatibility on the labels, because chemical fungicides can kill bio-agents. Mix seed and product evenly in a closed drum or plastic bag, wear gloves, dry the seed in shade and never use treated seed as food or feed.
