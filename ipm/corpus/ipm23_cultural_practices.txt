Title: Cultural practices that prevent pests
URL: https://www.fao.org/pest-and-pesticide-management/ipm/integrated-pest-management/en/
Org: FAO

Many pest problems can be prevented with good farming practice. Crop rotation breaks the life cycle of pests that attack only one crop family. Deep summer ploughing exposes soil pupae to heat and birds. Timely and synchronised sowing across a village avoids a long food supply for pests. Balanced fertiliser, especially not overusing nitrogen, makes plants less attractive to sucking pests. Proper spacing improves air flow and reduces humidity-loving diseases. Removing weeds and crop residues removes places where pests survive between seasons. These steps cost little and form the base of IPM.
