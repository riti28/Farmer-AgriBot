Title: Fall armyworm in Africa and the Americas
URL: https://www.cimmyt.org/
Org: CIMMYT (International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center)

Fall armyworm is native to the tropical and subtropical Americas, where it is a regular maize pest from the USA to Brazil and Argentina. It was first found in West and Central Africa in 2016 and spread across sub-Saharan Africa within two years, then reached Asia. CIMMYT and partners published an IPM guide for Africa that recommends regular scouting, early planting, push-pull intercropping (maize with a repellent legume such as desmodium and an attractive border grass such as napier or brachiaria), conserving natural enemies, and bio-pesticides. In the Americas, Bt maize is widely used, but fall armyworm has developed resistance to some Bt proteins there, so IPM is still needed.
