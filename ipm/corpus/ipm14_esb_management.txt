Title: IPM for early shoot borer in sugarcane
URL: https://www.uasd.edu/
Org: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Dharwad

To manage early shoot borer, plant early where possible so the young crop avoids the peak hot period, and use healthy setts. Trash mulching (spreading dry sugarcane leaves between rows) about 3 days after planting keeps the soil cool and moist and reduces attack. Light earthing up around 45 days covers the base of shoots. Remove and destroy dead hearts with larvae inside. Release Trichogramma chilonis egg parasitoid cards at regular intervals from about the first month. Intercropping with short-duration pulses also helps. If damage is above the threshold, use a recommended soil or spray application as per the university package of practices.
