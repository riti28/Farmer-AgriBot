Title: Trichoderma: a friendly fungus against soil diseases
URL: https://nbair.res.in/
Org: ICAR-National Bureau of Agricultural Insect Resources (NBAIR)

Trichoderma (for example Trichoderma viride and Trichoderma harzianum) is a beneficial fungus that lives in soil and on roots. It attacks and crowds out disease-causing fungi such as those causing root rot, damping-off, collar rot and wilt, and it can also promote root growth. It is used as a seed treatment (commonly about 4 to 10 grams per kilogram of seed, as per the product label), as a nursery bed or seedling root dip, and as soil application after multiplying it in well-decomposed farmyard manure for about a week. Trichoderma is a living organism, so keep the packet in a cool shaded place, use it before the expiry date and do not mix it with chemical fungicides. It prevents disease better than it cures a crop that is already badly infected.
