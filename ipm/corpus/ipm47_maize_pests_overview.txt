Title: Worms and other pests in maize
URL: https://iimr.res.in/
Org: ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR)

Caterpillars ("worms") are the most common pests of maize. Fall armyworm makes ragged holes in whorl leaves with sawdust-like droppings and has an inverted Y on the head. The spotted stem borer (Chilo partellus) makes rows of small holes in leaves, dead hearts in young plants and tunnels inside the stem. Pink stem borer attacks mainly in the winter (rabi) season. Cutworms cut young seedlings at ground level at night. Earworms (Helicoverpa) feed on cobs. Other pests include shoot fly in early sown crops, aphids and termites. For all of them, early scouting, timely sowing, removal of stubble, intercropping with pulses and conserving natural enemies come first; Bt or neem sprays work on young larvae, and chemicals are kept for when thresholds are crossed.
