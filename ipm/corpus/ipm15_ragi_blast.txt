Title: Blast disease of finger millet (ragi)
URL: https://www.millets.res.in/
Org: ICAR-Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR)

Blast, caused by the fungus Pyricularia grisea (also called Magnaporthe), is the most serious disease of finger millet. It appears as spindle-shaped grey spots with brown edges on leaves (leaf blast), as black lesions on the neck below the earhead (neck blast), and as damaged fingers (finger blast). Neck and finger blast cause the biggest yield loss because grains do not fill. The disease spreads fast in cool nights with high humidity, long leaf wetness and intermittent rain. Infected seed and crop residues carry the fungus to the next season.
