Title: Conserving natural enemies in the field
URL: https://nbair.res.in/
Org: ICAR-National Bureau of Agricultural Insect Resources (NBAIR)

Natural enemies are the farmer's free helpers. Predators such as spiders, lady beetles, ground beetles and dragonflies eat pests; parasitoid wasps lay eggs in or on pests; and diseases of insects kill them in humid weather. To conserve them: avoid broad-spectrum sprays especially in the early crop, grow flowering plants like marigold, sunflower or cowpea on bunds to give them nectar, and put up bird perches (about 10 per acre) so birds can pick caterpillars. A simple ratio check, such as one spider for every few hoppers, can show that the natural enemies are already controlling the pest and no spray is needed.
