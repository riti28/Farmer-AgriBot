Title: Pink bollworm management practices
URL: https://cicr.org.in/
Org: ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR)

Key IPM steps against pink bollworm: sow short-duration varieties on time, avoid extending the crop with late irrigation, and end the crop by December-January so the pest has no food to carry over. After harvest, remove and destroy cotton stalks and fallen bolls, and do not store seed cotton near fields. Pick and destroy rosette flowers. Mating disruption using pheromone dispensers spread across the field confuses male moths and reduces mating. Release of Trichogramma egg parasitoids supports natural control. Use insecticides only after the threshold is crossed and rotate products from different chemical groups.
