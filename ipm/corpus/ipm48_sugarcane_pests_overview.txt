Title: Insect pests of sugarcane
URL: https://sugarcane.icar.gov.in/
Org: ICAR-Sugarcane Breeding Institute (SBI)

Sugarcane is attacked by several insects. Early shoot borer kills young shoots (dead hearts) in the first months. Internode borer bores into the cane stalk during grand growth, causing reddened tunnels and lower sugar. Top shoot borer attacks the growing top, causing bunchy top. Woolly aphid forms white woolly colonies under leaves in humid weather. Scale insects form grey crusts on the stalk, and mealybugs hide behind leaf sheaths. Underground pests include white grubs and termites. IPM for sugarcane combines healthy, treated setts, trash mulching, timely earthing up, detrashing of dry leaves, removal of late shoots, release of Trichogramma parasitoids, and regular monitoring; chemicals are used only for the pest that is actually found above its threshold.
