Title: Thrips: tiny insects that scrape leaves
URL: https://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/
Org: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU Agritech Portal)

Thrips are very small slender insects, pale yellow to dark brown, that hide in flowers, buds and leaf folds. They scrape the leaf surface and suck the juice, leaving silvery streaks or patches, curled or cup-shaped leaves and scarred fruits. They attack cotton, chilli, onion, groundnut and many vegetables, and some species spread tospoviruses such as peanut bud necrosis. Thrips increase in dry weather; rain and overhead irrigation reduce them. Blue sticky traps catch adult thrips and show when numbers rise. Remove weeds, avoid water stress, and use neem-based sprays or a recommended insecticide aimed at the growing tips when damage spreads.
