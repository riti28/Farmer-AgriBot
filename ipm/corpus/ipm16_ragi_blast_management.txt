Title: Managing finger millet (ragi) blast (example from Karnataka, India)
URL: https://www.uasbangalore.edu.in/
Org: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Bengaluru

Growing a blast-resistant ragi variety is the cheapest protection; UAS Bengaluru has released varieties such as GPU 28 that are popular for their blast resistance. Other steps: use clean seed, treat seed with a bio-agent like Pseudomonas fluorescens or a recommended fungicide, avoid very late sowing, avoid excess nitrogen, and remove infected crop residues. When leaf blast is seen in humid weather, and again at earhead emergence to protect against neck blast, a recommended fungicide spray may be given as advised by the local agriculture officer.
