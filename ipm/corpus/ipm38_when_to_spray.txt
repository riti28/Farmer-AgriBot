Title: When should you spray a pesticide?
URL: https://ncipm.icar.gov.in/
Org: ICAR-National Centre for Integrated Pest Management (NCIPM)

Spray only when field scouting shows the pest has reached the economic threshold level for that crop and stage, not by calendar or because a neighbour sprayed. First identify the pest correctly and check whether natural enemies are already controlling it. If a spray is needed, choose a product registered for that crop and pest, and prefer bio-pesticides or selective products. Spray in the early morning or late evening when wind is calm and bees are less active. Do not spray before expected rain, in strong wind or in the hot midday sun. Use clean water, the right nozzle and the full spray volume so the target part of the plant is covered. Wait the number of days given on the label before harvesting. After spraying, check the field again in a few days to see whether it worked.
