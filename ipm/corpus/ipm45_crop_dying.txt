Title: Why is my crop dying? Wilting and sudden drying
URL: https://www.plantwise.org/
Org: CABI (PlantwisePlus programme)

When plants wilt or dry, first check the pattern and the roots. If the whole field wilts in the afternoon and recovers at night, the cause is often water stress. If single plants or patches die, pull out a plant: rotten or brown roots and stem base suggest root rot or collar rot; brown streaks inside the stem when it is cut suggest a wilt disease such as Fusarium wilt; eaten roots or larvae in the soil point to white grubs, termites or cutworms; a hole in the stem with frass points to a stem borer. Waterlogging, salty soil and herbicide damage can also kill plants. Take a whole plant with roots and some soil to your nearest KVK, extension office or plant clinic for a diagnosis before spraying anything.
