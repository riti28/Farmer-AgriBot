Title: Safe and judicious use of pesticides
URL: https://ppqs.gov.in/
Org: Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (Govt. of India)

In IPM a chemical pesticide is the last option. Use only a pesticide registered by the national pesticide authority (in India, the Central Insecticides Board and Registration Committee) for that crop and pest, at the dose on the label. Read the label colour triangle that shows toxicity. Wear gloves, mask and full clothes while mixing and spraying, do not eat or smoke while spraying, and spray in calm weather in the morning or evening. Keep the waiting period between spraying and harvest. Never reuse empty pesticide containers; destroy them safely. Rotate chemical groups to slow resistance and avoid mixing many products in one tank.
