Title: Yellow leaves: pest, disease or nutrient deficiency?
URL: https://www.agritech.tnau.ac.in/
Org: Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU Agritech Portal)

Yellowing leaves can have many causes, so look closely before buying any chemical. Nutrient deficiency usually shows a regular pattern over the whole field: nitrogen deficiency yellows the older lower leaves first; zinc, iron or sulphur deficiency often yellows the younger leaves or shows yellow stripes between veins. Pests leave signs you can see: insects or eggs under leaves, honeydew, holes or speckling, and patches that spread; for example, planthoppers or jassids cause yellow then brown drying. Diseases often show spots, lesions with borders, mould, rotting or wilting, and may start in patches. Waterlogging, drought and herbicide drift can also cause yellowing. A soil test, a leaf sample to the nearest KVK or plant clinic, and checking under the leaves with a hand lens help find the true cause.
