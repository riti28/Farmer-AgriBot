Title: Which spray for cotton? Know the pest first
URL: https://cicr.org.in/
Org: ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR)

There is no single best spray for cotton, because cotton has two groups of pests that need different products. Sucking pests (aphids, jassids, thrips, whitefly, mealybug) are common in the first 60 to 80 days; they are managed with seed treatment, sticky traps, conserving predators and neem or selective insecticides only above the threshold. Bollworms (pink bollworm, American bollworm, spotted bollworm) attack squares, flowers and bolls later; they are monitored with pheromone traps and boll checks and managed with destruction of rosette flowers, mating disruption, Trichogramma releases, Bt or neem early, and a recommended insecticide when thresholds are crossed. Avoid broad-spectrum sprays and mixtures early in the season; they kill natural enemies and often lead to whitefly or bollworm outbreaks later. Ask the local agriculture officer which products are recommended in your area this season.
