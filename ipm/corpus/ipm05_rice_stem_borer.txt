Title: Yellow stem borer in rice
URL: https://icar-nrri.in/
Org: ICAR-National Rice Research Institute (NRRI)

The yellow stem borer caterpillar bores into the rice stem. In young plants this kills the central shoot, which turns brown and pulls out easily (dead heart). At the earhead stage it causes empty, white panicles (white ear). Moths lay egg masses on leaf tips. IPM steps: clip seedling leaf tips before transplanting to remove egg masses, plough stubble after harvest to kill hibernating larvae, avoid excess nitrogen, and use pheromone traps for monitoring and mass trapping. Release of Trichogramma japonicum egg parasitoids at weekly intervals helps. Action thresholds often used are about 10% dead hearts in the vegetative stage, 2% white ears at flowering, or one egg mass per square metre. Only then use a recommended granule or spray.
