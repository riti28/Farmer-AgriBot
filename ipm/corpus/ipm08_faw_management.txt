Title: Managing fall armyworm on maize with IPM
URL: https://openknowledge.fao.org/handle/20.500.14283/i8665en
Org: FAO (FAW guide for farmer field schools)

FAO advises farmers to manage fall armyworm by first understanding its life cycle and natural enemies. Useful steps: sow on time and at the same time as neighbours, intercrop maize with legumes such as cowpea or pulses, keep fields free of weeds, and hand-pick egg masses and young larvae. Many parasitoid wasps, earwigs and ants attack FAW, so protect them by avoiding unnecessary sprays. Putting sand, soil or a little wood ash into the whorl can kill or block young larvae. Heavy rain can also wash out or drown young larvae. Neem-based or Bt bio-pesticides work best on small larvae. Chemical insecticides should be the last option, applied into the whorl and only when damage crosses the local threshold.
